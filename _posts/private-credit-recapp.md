> **Executive Briefing Memorandum**  
> Apollo and Ares completed a priority restructuring for CloudMetrics, subordinating mezzanine creditors following covenant breach. Coupon resets to SOFR + 750 bps with equity warrant dilution reaching 22% of total capitalization.

---

### The Capital Structure Re-Order

A syndication club spearheaded by direct lending vehicles managed by Apollo Global Management and Ares Management has executed a private, out-of-court uptier exchange on CloudMetrics Inc.'s **$650 million** first-lien term loan facility.

The transaction highlights the accelerating frequency of aggressive Liability Management Exercises (LMEs) inside non-bank direct lending portfolios. As enterprise SaaS valuations compressed from historical peaks of 18x ARR to 6.2x ARR, CloudMetrics tripped its recurring-revenue net leverage ratio covenant (pegged at 4.75x) for two consecutive fiscal quarters.

### Covenant Priming & Subordination Structure

Under terms negotiated over an intense three-week standstill window:
- **Super-Priority Tranche**: $180 million of new money injected by the participating majority lenders primes existing first-lien claims with super-senior security status.
- **Spread Adjustment**: The base coupon resets from SOFR + 475 bps to SOFR + 750 bps (comprising 550 bps cash interest and 200 bps payment-in-kind).
- **Equity Dilution & Warrants**: Junior debtholders and original equity sponsors suffer 22% immediate equity dilution through penny-warrant allocations granted directly to the restructuring syndicate.

### Institutional Credit Ramifications

Unlike syndicated broadly syndicated loans (BSL) where S&P/Moody's ratings provide public oversight, private credit restructuring agreements remain closely held within credit committee memorandums. 

Legal advisers at Gibson Dunn and Kirkland & Ellis represent competing creditor classes, with excluded minority lenders evaluating whether the indenture language allowed cross-series debt exchanges without unanimous lender consent under Section 9.02 of the Model Credit Agreement.
