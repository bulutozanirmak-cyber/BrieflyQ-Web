> **Executive Briefing Memorandum (BLUF)**  
> The Direct Lending Syndicate, through the New York Supreme Court Commercial Division, enforced strict collateral restraints on a $1.2 billion private credit facility. Invoking N.Y. U.C.C. § 9-601 and LSTA Model Credit Agreement Section 2.05, lenders capped synthetic PIK interest at 4.5% and forced immediate cash sweeps, widening sponsor mezzanine yield spreads by 380 basis points.

---

### Commercial Foreclosure & Direct Lending Precedent: Index No. 652940/2026

In an escalating clash between top-tier direct lenders and private equity sponsors, an institutional senior secured syndicate led by institutional private debt managers filed emergency enforcement petitions in the **New York Supreme Court Commercial Division**, exercising default rights over a **$1.2 billion** unitranche financing facility.

The legal confrontation centers on the borrower’s unilateral election of a "synthetic payment-in-kind" (PIK) interest toggle intended to preserve operational liquidity amidst elevated SOFR base rates. The syndicate successfully established that recurring interest capitalization triggered covenant violations under contractual minimum fixed-charge coverage ratios.

### Statutory Enforcement Under Article 9 & Intercreditor Covenants

Pursuant to the New York Uniform Commercial Code and standard institutional Loan Syndications and Trading Association (LSTA) conventions, the court sustained the syndicate’s immediate protective remedies:
- **Article 9 Deposit Account Control Enforcement**: Lenders exercised activation notices under Deposit Account Control Agreements (DACAs) pursuant to N.Y. U.C.C. § 9-104 and § 9-607, directing corporate treasury revenues straight into an administrative lockbox account.
- **PIK Interest Cap & Reversion**: The court barred the debtor from capitalizing more than 450 basis points of total margin, compelling the remaining 650 basis points of the SOFR + 650 spread to be disbursed monthly in hard cash.
- **Junior Sponsor Equity Subordination**: Strict equity-cure rights were frozen, precluding the financial sponsor from contributing subordinated shareholder notes to cosmetically satisfy quarterly EBITDA maintenance tests.

### Liquidity Impacts & Private Credit Yield Re-Pricing

The Commercial Division’s swift enforcement represents an inflection point in the $1.7 trillion private credit market:
- **Mezzanine Yield Spreads**: Subordinated mezzanine and junior holdco paper widened by 380 basis points, reflecting heightened loss-given-default assumptions across recurring-revenue software credits.
- **Unitranche Loan Pricing**: Senior direct lending spreads on enterprise software collateral adjusted upwards by 75 bps to compensate for intensified restructuring litigation risk.
- **Waterfall Restructuring**: The sponsor was ordered to inject $185 million in unencumbered common equity within 45 days or face UCC non-judicial foreclosure of the corporate equity pledge.
